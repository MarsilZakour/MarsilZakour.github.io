ADL4D: Towards A Contextually Rich Dataset for 4D Activities of Daily Living

Marsil Zakour et al.
Technical University of Munich
Paper (under review)Data (coming soon)adl4d.github.io

Abstract

Understanding hand-object interaction (HOI) in daily activities requires structure over long horizons: objects are picked up, used, shared and released according to a plan, and concurrent hands serve different roles. Existing datasets for 4D HOI (3D HOI over time) record short, single-subject, single-object clips and cannot represent this structure. We introduce ADL4D, a multi-view RGB-D dataset in which up to two subjects use and share multiple objects during dining activities (1833 multi-view frames per sequence on average; 1.1M images in total), annotated by an automatic multi-view hand-tracking pipeline with marker-based object capture. ADL4D provides per-hand, frame-wise verb-noun action segments over full activities, together with 3D hand poses and 6-DoF poses for every object. Hand mesh recovery and hand-object reconstruction benchmarks show that ADL4D is challenging zero-shot yet effective for training. We focus on the two tasks these annotations enable: per-hand action segmentation, where conditioning on object geometry yields large gains, and object-pose forecasting from hand motion alone, where the recovered action segments improve prediction.
