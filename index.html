About

I am a Ph.D. candidate at the Technical University of Munich working on 3D computer vision and machine learning. My research focuses on 4D hand–object interaction: reconstructing hands and objects in 3D and over time from video, modelling contact, and building the capture systems and datasets behind them. I am broadly interested in real-time perception, differentiable optimization, and embodied AI.

Before my Ph.D., I competed at the ACM-ICPC World Finals and spent years coaching and judging competitive-programming contests.

Software

parafit — a pluggable analytical solver for parametric model fitting
Standalone open-source library: batched differentiable Levenberg–Marquardt with analytic Jacobians (≈12–16× faster than autodiff) for hands, bodies, and 6-DoF objects.
github.com/marcilzakour/parafit

Systems

  • Real-time hand–object tracker — live multi-view RGB 6-DoF hand and object tracking on a single consumer GPU: detection → triangulation → optimization-in-the-loop.
  • Multi-view capture & annotation pipeline — 8-camera rig, 0.8 px calibration, MANO-based auto-annotation with contact refinement; the data engine behind our dataset work.
  • HOIsim — simulator generating realistic synthetic 3D human–object interaction data for perception training and sim-to-real studies.

Demo videos are available on request and linked from my CV.
